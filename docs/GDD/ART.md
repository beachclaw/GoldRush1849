# Art Direction — GoldRush1849

## Visual Style
Low-poly 3D. Clean geometry, expressive color, no photorealism.
Readable silhouettes. Intentionally simple — charm over complexity.

## Palette Evolution

### Phase 1 — The Wilderness (1848)
- Warm golds and ambers — morning light on the American River
- Deep forest greens — untouched Sierra Nevada
- Earthy browns and terracotta — exposed rock and riverbank
- Dusty whites — canvas tents, sun-bleached driftwood
- Sky: hazy blue-gold, California morning

### Phase 2 — The Camp (1850s)
- More wooden structures — warm ochres
- Smoke from fires and forges — grey-blue haze creeps in
- The river runs muddier — you've disturbed it
- Colors warm but more muted — the wild edge is fading

### Phase 3 — The Operation (1860s)
- Industrial greys and blacks emerging — machinery, coal smoke
- Scar lines in the landscape — hydraulic erosion, open shafts
- Contrast: company buildings vs. workers' worn shacks
- Gold still visible but harder to see — obscured by industry

## Lighting
- Dynamic time of day
- Dawn: magic hour, golden mist off the river
- Midday: harsh California sun, deep shadows
- Dusk: melancholy warmth, long shadows
- Night: campfire pools of orange in deep blue dark
- Underground: torch/lantern pools in absolute darkness

## Camera Evolution
| Phase | Style | Feel |
|-------|-------|------|
| Prospector | Close, shoulder-height | Intimate, you feel small |
| Claim | Medium pull-back | See your camp in context |
| Operation | Isometric-ish | See your footprint on the land |
| Tycoon (v2) | God-view | Survey your empire |

## Character Design
- Simple, readable low-poly silhouettes
- **Player (Sourdough):** worn hat, rolled sleeves, suspenders
- **Workers:** differentiated by role — miner, foreman, engineer
- **NPCs:** merchants, rivals, locals — each era introduces new archetypes

## Environmental Storytelling
The land itself tells the story of progress:
- Early: pristine river, dense forest, birdsong
- Mid: muddy banks, stumps where trees were, worn paths
- Late: scarred hillsides, hydraulic erosion channels, smoke on the horizon
